Hong Kong

Hong Kong is a city I remember most vividly at night.

During the hot daytime, I often found myself escaping into malls, restaurants, and cafés, watching the energy of the city from a little distance. Once the heat faded, I enjoyed walking through different neighborhoods and exploring the city almost without a destination.

The contrast between daytime Hong Kong and its streets after dark was one of the things I found most fascinating. Familiar places could suddenly feel completely different under the lights of the city.

Singapore

Singapore is a place I have visited several times, and some of my favorite memories there involve the people I met along the way.

On more than one occasion, I ended up sharing a beer and satay with Korean travelers I had only just met, talking about the trips we had already taken and making plans for places to visit next.

I also visited Singapore for the Formula 1 race with friends, which showed me a much louder and more energetic side of the city. Later, I enjoyed exploring some of Singapore’s quieter cocktail bars, where the atmosphere felt almost entirely different from the crowds surrounding the race.

For me, travel is not only about seeing new places. It is also about collecting small encounters, conversations, and scenes that would be difficult to experience in ordinary life.
