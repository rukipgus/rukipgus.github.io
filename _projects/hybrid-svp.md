**Current Research · Quantum Optimization / Lattice Problems**

## Overview

I am currently investigating hybrid quantum-classical methods for the **Shortest Vector Problem (SVP)** on structured lattice instances.

The project explores whether quantum optimization can provide a useful computational role when combined with classical methods under realistic resource constraints.

As this is an ongoing research project, technical details and experimental results are not publicly disclosed at this time.
